ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Making Student Elections More Accessible

The FUTA E-Voting Platform is designed to simplify student election participation through a digital voting experience.

By providing convenient access to election information and a structured voting process, the platform aims to encourage participation, accountability and transparency in student elections.
